### 02. I S Market – E-Commerce Product Catalog & Interactive Shopping Cart

**A Responsive Online Shopping Store**

I S Market is a modern e-commerce storefront developed to demonstrate product catalog management, interactive shopping cart functionality and dynamic price calculations.

The application integrates FakeStoreAPI for product data and includes additional illustrative product listings to demonstrate a broader shopping experience across multiple departments.

**Key Features**
- Dynamic product fetching using FakeStoreAPI.
- Product cards displaying images, prices, ratings and badges.
- Product search, category filtering and sorting.
- Multiple shopping departments and product collections.
- Interactive shopping cart with quantity management.
- Automatic subtotal, tax, shipping and total calculations.
- Customer details and delivery address form.
- Simulated checkout with order confirmation.
- Order history and Buy Again functionality.
- Local storage for cart, theme and order data.
- Responsive layout with light and dark themes.

**Technologies:** HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · FakeStoreAPI · Browser localStorage

**Project Folder:** `Task-2-Ecommerce-Store/`
